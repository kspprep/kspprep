KSPPREP DEVELOPMENT ROADMAP

Build the foundation.

KSPPrep is being developed in deliberate stages. The current foundation focuses on structured training, testing, results, persistence, and account-backed progress. The next headline upgrade is planned around GPS-powered run tracking and pace.

VERSION 1.0

TRAIN • TEST • TRACK

01

The v1.0 foundation is designed to make structured preparation easier to follow, preserve progress when training is interrupted, and give users a clear view of their work over time.

12-WEEK PROGRAMStructured daily workouts with progressive training demands.
TEST CENTERPractice and record test-focused events and supplemental preparation.
RESULTS + PERSONAL BESTSReview saved performance and recent training activity.
WORKOUT IN PROGRESSSave an unfinished session and return without starting over.
OFFLINE PERSISTENCESupported training data remains available through temporary connectivity loss.
CROSS-DEVICE SYNCSigned-in training state synchronizes across supported devices.
ACCOUNT CONTROLSReset training data, sign out, privacy controls, and permanent account deletion.
WORKOUT PLAYERGet Ready → In Progress → Recovery with timers, sets, and clear next actions.
